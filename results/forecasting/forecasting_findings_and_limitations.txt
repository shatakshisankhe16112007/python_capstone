MEMBER 3 - TIME SERIES FORECASTING REPORT

1. SYLLABUS METHODOLOGY (EXP 12 STATSMODELS TIME SERIES)
- Series Formulation: Annual median yield across reporting Indian states and seasons (1997-2019)
  formulated as a Pandas DatetimeIndex series for Rice, Wheat, Maize, and Sugarcane.
- Stationarity Verification:
  Augmented Dickey-Fuller (ADF) testing revealed that raw annual series possess stochastic upward trends
  (p >= 0.05 for Rice, Wheat, and Maize). First-order differencing (d=1) stabilized the series mean,
  achieving stationarity as prescribed in Experiment 12 Task 1.
- Smoothing Analysis:
  A 3-year Simple Moving Average (SMA) and Exponentially Weighted Moving Average (EWMA, span=3) were compared.
  EWMA demonstrated lower Mean Absolute Deviation from the original series, adapting more responsively to recent shocks.
- Model Specification & Selection:
  Candidate ARIMA(p, d, q) specifications were systematically benchmarked against Naive Last Value and
  Linear Trend models across a 4-year holdout window (2016-2019) using Akaike Information Criterion (AIC) and RMSE.

5. ACADEMIC LIMITATIONS
1. Small Sample Size: With 23 historical yearly observations (1997-2019), asymptotic statistical properties
   of ADF test statistics and high-order ARMA parameters are constrained.
2. Short Holdout Window: The 4-year evaluation period (2016-2019) provides indicative rather than exhaustive validation.
3. Exogenous Shocks: ARIMA relies on internal autoregressive and moving average dynamics; unpredictable monsoon droughts,
   floods, pest outbreaks, or minimum support price policy changes cannot be anticipated from historical lags alone.
4. Aggregation Level: Forecasts describe typical median state yield rather than specific district microclimates.
